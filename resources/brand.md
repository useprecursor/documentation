# Brand and channels

## The name

A precursor is the thing that comes before. Private markets are where public companies come from, and Precursor is built for that stage: the years when a company is growing, raising and rewarding the people who built it, before it ever lists.

## Writing the name

* **Precursor** is one word with a single capital P. It isn't set in all capitals in running text, and it isn't abbreviated.
* Product names take the name in front: **Precursor Console**, **Precursor Portfolio**, **Precursor Explorer**, **Precursor API**. After the first mention on a page, "Console", "Portfolio" and "Explorer" are fine on their own.
* Tokens are **Precursor Share Tokens**, or "share tokens" after the first mention. Avoid "coins" and "crypto stocks".
* Precursor's relationship to the network is always written as **built on Robinhood Chain**. Precursor is an independent company. Never describe it as a partner of, endorsed by or affiliated with Robinhood, or with any company that hasn't onboarded.

## Voice

Precursor writes like infrastructure: precise, calm and technical, and credible enough for a CFO to forward to the board.

1. **Precise over promotional.** Say "holding periods enforced at the contract level", not "fully secure". Name the contract, the event or the rule.
2. **Show the mechanism.** Use interfaces, tables, transaction hashes and Explorer links instead of adjectives.
3. **Respect the reader.** Explain what an action does before someone takes it, in plain language, and say what can't be undone.
4. **No crypto vernacular.** No "moon", "degen", "airdrop" or memecoin imagery. Use "stock tokens", "share classes", "settlement" and "holders".
5. **Plain sentences.** Short paragraphs, active voice, one idea at a time. No exclamation marks.

### Words we use and words we avoid

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| Share token, stock token | Coin, crypto stock |
| Holder, shareholder | Degen, whale, bag holder |
| Liquidity Window | Unlock event, pump |
| Settlement, clearing price | Moon, price action |
| Issuer-sponsored | Official, partnered |
| Eligible investors | Everyone, anyone, the masses |

## Sample copy

* **Headline:** Equity infrastructure for the companies that come next.
* **Subhead:** Precursor lets private companies issue stock tokens on Robinhood Chain, with every issuance, transfer and corporate action settled transparently on-chain.
* **Calls to action:** Start tokenizing · Read the documentation · View on Explorer
* **Trust line:** Issuer-sponsored · ERC-3643 · Settled on Ethereum via Robinhood Chain · Audited
* **One line:** Precursor is to private-company equity what payments infrastructure is to commerce: the programmable layer that turns a slow, manual process into an API.
